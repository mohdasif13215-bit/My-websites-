Save Environment, Save Future

Once there was a small village surrounded by green forests and clean rivers. People lived happily because they respected nature. They planted trees, saved water, and never polluted the air.

One day, people from nearby cities started cutting trees and throwing waste. Slowly, the air became polluted and water became dirty. A young boy stood up and taught everyone that nature is our life.

The villagers understood their mistake. They cleaned the river, planted more trees, and used eco-friendly products. Nature smiled again, and the village became green and healthy.

Moral: Protect nature today to live a better life tomorrow.
